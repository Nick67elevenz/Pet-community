Which Pet Is Right for You?

Different pets have different needs. Compare these animals before deciding which one might suit your lifestyle.

Pet Comparison Chart
Pet Average Lifespan Size Daily Care Activity Level
Dog Varies by breed Small to large High High
Cat Often 12–18 years Small to medium Moderate Moderate
Rabbit Often 8–12 years Small to medium Moderate to high Moderate

Things to Consider

  • How much time can you spend caring for a pet?
  • How much space does the animal need?
  • Can you provide suitable food and veterinary care?
  • Is everyone in your household comfortable with the pet?

Remember: Every animal deserves a safe, loving home and proper care.
